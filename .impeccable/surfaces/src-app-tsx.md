# Surface: storefront (single page)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Pakistani shoppers on phones, half arriving from Instagram/WhatsApp, half by word of mouth. Job: trust the herbal range and send a COD order on WhatsApp. Proof on hand: the real labels and product photos only; no reviews or counts exist.

Direction pinned by the brief and user: "the label come to life". The roll was not run because the direction is pinned.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the Nana Herbiotics label unfolded. The label's arched gold-ruled panel, corner herb engravings, crest and badge seals become the page's frame system. It refuses the split hero with a headline left and a product right, and the row of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Forest green ground (#173326 to #0E2018) with a faint engraved-herb texture. Antique gold double hairlines with an arched top and drawn corner sprigs. Cinzel caps for display (it matches the label lettering), EB Garamond for story text and Figtree for UI. Pure white wells hold the products, and hibiscus pink appears only on the Hibiscus panel.

STORY: In one view: herbal hair and skin care from a small Pakistani maker, gentle and paraben free, and you can add to cart now. The scroll walks through the shelf (shampoos, conditioner, skin, soaps), then the promises, the story, and checkout on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A tall gold arch frame is centred, filling about 70% of the height, and a 3D bottle turns inside it on a turntable. "Botanical Care, Naturally" in Cinzel sits across the arch's foot. A label plaque below holds the name, size, price, Add to cart and Shop Now. Chevrons swap the bottle between the three hair bottles.

FORM: The label as page frame (the pinned world). The signature interaction is the 3D turntable that swaps bottles with real label textures and has working add-to-cart. Motion grammar: gold rules draw in, and herbs drift in depth. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
